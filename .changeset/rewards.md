Rewards for keeping at it. Today shows your streak and the day's goal. A new Progress screen shows the streak and its freezes, a heat map of practice by the day, what you have learnt, how well each passage is held, and badges, from a first passage learnt to a passage held for a year. Missing a day is forgiving: a freeze earned by practice covers it, a long day soon after mends it, and a weekly rest day can be chosen in Settings. A passage's page marks the words you miss most.
