Connect a storage account from Settings, and choose between the versions of a passage changed in
two places at once. Settings offers Dropbox, Google Drive or OneDrive, says how syncing stands,
syncs on request and disconnects. A new screen shows each conflict side by side, with undo. The
backup reminder stays quiet while the library syncs, and the CSP lets the app reach the providers.
