Keeping your library safe, and getting around by keyboard. Once you have a passage, the app asks the browser to keep your library for good, and Settings says whether it has agreed. Today reminds you to export a backup when the last one is a week old and your library has changed since. You can put the reminder off. The offer to install the app now waits until there is a passage to keep. Press Ctrl+K (⌘K on a Mac) for a command palette: go to any screen, start today's practice, add a passage, export a backup, or practise or edit the passage you are reading.
