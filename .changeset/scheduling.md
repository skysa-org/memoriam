Practise every day: Today shows what is due and how many new words there are to learn, and Start runs the day's session. Learnt parts come back for review on a spaced schedule (FSRS); a part forgotten at review is learnt again in the same session. Each new part is joined to the one before it, and each section learnt in is run through at the end. Settings choose how well to remember, how many new words a day, the longest wait between reviews, and whether slips of the finger on a touch keyboard are forgiven.
