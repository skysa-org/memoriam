Back up your library and bring passages in. Settings can now export the whole library, with your progress and practice records, as a ZIP of markdown files that opens anywhere. Import takes that ZIP back, on this device or another, or any markdown or text files. A passage you already have keeps its words and gains the progress made on the other copy. One whose words differ is added beside yours as a copy, so nothing is written over.
